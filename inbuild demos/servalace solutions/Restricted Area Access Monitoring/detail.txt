This system allows users to customize any selected location as an unauthorized area. Once the area is marked, the system can track and monitor whether a person, animal, pet, or any object enters or trespasses into that restricted zone.

In this demo, we show a sample of how the restricted area can be customized smoothly and at a micro level. If someone or something crosses the selected boundary, the system can detect the trespassing activity and trigger an alert or alarm.

This approach is flexible and can be adapted to different real-world solutions, such as security monitoring, pet tracking, workplace safety, and restricted-area surveillance.
