Adaptive Re-Identification Tracking (ARIT) is an intelligent vision-tracking approach designed to continuously monitor objects even under challenging real-world conditions such as occlusions, motion blur, rapid movement, frame drops, camera shake, or temporary disappearance from the scene. Instead of treating a missing object as a completely new entity, the system maintains a temporal memory of the object’s visual appearance, motion pattern, trajectory, and behavioral characteristics. When the object reappears, the system adaptively re-identifies it and seamlessly continues the tracking process without losing continuity.) to track objects accurately across frames during fast motion and occlusion. Useful for surveillance, sports analytics, robotics, and autonomous systems.
